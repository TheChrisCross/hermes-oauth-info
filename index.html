A personal assistant
for Gmail.

Hermes Gmail MCP connects a privately operated AI assistant to Gmail through Google-authorized access. It helps the account holder review messages, summarize relevant information, and manage email tasks.

What the integration does

With the account holder’s permission, the assistant can search and read email, identify actionable messages, and assist with drafting or other authorized mailbox actions. Access depends on the Google permissions granted and the assistant’s configuration.

This is an AI-agent integration. Google Workspace access may use the Gmail API or Workspace MCP where available and authorized.

Access stays with the account holder

This website is an information page, not a sign-up service. It does not provide a login, collect credentials, or grant anyone access to a mailbox. The integration is intended for personal use, not a public email service.

Understand the data handling

Email information may be processed by configured AI services and retained in assistant history or memory. Not all processing is necessarily local. Read the privacy notice before authorizing access.
