Data handling

Privacy notice.

This notice covers the personal-use Hermes Gmail MCP integration and this static information website.

1. Google data accessed

The integration accesses only accounts authorized by the account holder. Depending on granted permissions and the task, it may access email addresses and headers, subjects, message bodies, labels, mailbox metadata, drafts, and attachments. Permissions may also allow authorized draft creation, sending, or mailbox changes. Granting a permission does not mean every message or attachment is read.

2. Why the data is used

Google user data is used to provide requested assistant functionality: searching and reviewing email, summarizing content, identifying relevant tasks, drafting responses, and performing authorized mailbox actions. Relevant information may be passed to other configured assistant workflows to carry out those tasks.

The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its purpose is personal assistance, not training generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Use of Google user data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. AI processing and service providers

Relevant email content, metadata, summaries, or extracted facts may be sent to configured AI inference providers, routing services, or memory-processing services when needed for the requested functionality. Processing may take place outside the local device. Provider-specific processing and retention depend on the service and its settings; this notice does not promise that every provider retains no data.

The account holder controls the assistant configuration and should review the terms and privacy settings of enabled providers before allowing sensitive content to be processed.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored by the private assistant installation so it can access Google services and renew access tokens. Message content, results, summaries, and derived facts may be retained in assistant session history, operational records, or configured memory stores, including backups. This static website does not receive or store those credentials or email records.

There is no automatic fixed deletion period promised by this integration. Retained data remains until removed through the assistant’s history, memory, log, or backup controls, subject to any enabled service provider’s retention rules.

5. Control, revocation, and deletion

The account holder may stop the integration and revoke its access using Google Account third-party access controls. Revoking access stops future authorized requests but does not automatically erase previously retained history, memory, logs, or backups. Those records must be removed separately from the configured assistant and storage services.

Requests to inspect or delete retained assistant data can be made through the private assistant interface. The configured support contact is also available on the Google authorization screen; no personal contact information is published on this website.

6. Security and public access

Google API communications use HTTPS. Access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, and mailbox content are not published in this information website or its source repository. Access to the private assistant’s credentials and records must be restricted to authorized users. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. This information website

The website has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party font requests. It does not intentionally set cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary HTTP request information, such as IP addresses and user-agent strings, under its own privacy policy. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Changes

This notice should be updated when the integration’s data practices change. Review it before granting or renewing access. It describes the integration, not a guarantee about all configurations of the underlying assistant software.
